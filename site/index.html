About me

I'm a Realtor who also manages rentals.

Sandra Wortham started Wortham Property Management to take good care of owners' properties and the people who live in them. I joined the team in 2022, and I've been part of that work every day since.

Managing homes has taught me things most agents only learn after closing: what repairs actually cost, what rents a neighborhood will support, which houses hold tenants and which sit empty. When I help you buy, sell or rent, you get that experience.

I care most about honest answers, quick replies, and relationships that last longer than one transaction.
