Wichita Falls rental market

Why investors look at Wichita Falls

Steady military demand

Sheppard Air Force Base brings a steady flow of service members and families who need rental housing, many paying with a housing allowance.

Student & staff housing

Midwestern State University and the local hospitals and employers create demand for smaller units close to campus and work.

Affordable entry prices

Home prices here are low compared with the state's big metros, so rent-to-price ratios can work in ways they no longer do in Dallas or Austin.

Common questions

Investor FAQ

Do I have to use WPM for management if you help me buy?

No. You're free to self-manage or use anyone you like. Most of my investor clients do choose WPM, since the same people who helped analyze the deal end up running it.

What kind of properties make sense in Wichita Falls?

It depends on your goals. Three-bedroom single-family homes in established neighborhoods tend to lease quickly and hold tenants longer. Duplexes and small multifamily can cash flow better but need more hands-on management. I'll walk you through the trade-offs.

Can you help if I live out of state?

Yes. Many of our owners live out of town or are military families who kept a home after a PCS. Showings, inspections and closings can be handled remotely.

I already own a rental. Can WPM take it over?

Yes, including properties with a tenant already in place. We'll review the current lease and deposit records, then take over from there.

How much does management cost?

It depends on the property and the services you need. Contact me for a free proposal with exact numbers.
